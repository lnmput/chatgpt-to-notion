A local-first journey

When we first started building AI Chat Backup, the goal was simple: we wanted a way to save our own conversations with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini without locking them into proprietary history lists or exposing them to third-party database servers. We believed in a local-first, privacy-respecting workflow that lets you sync useful information directly to destinations you control—Notion, Obsidian, or clean local Markdown files.

Seeing over 10,000 active users rely on this extension every week tells us we aren't alone in wanting to keep control over our digital thoughts and workspace research.

Your privacy is core to what we do. AI Chat Backup operates locally in your browser and only communicates with destinations you authorize. We do not have backend databases or tracking servers, ensuring your chat content stays yours.

What's next for AI Chat Backup

As the AI space rapidly evolves, we are focused on keeping our integrations robust, stable, and simple. Here is what we are focusing on for future updates:

  • Robust Parsing: AI platforms update their DOM elements frequently. We are constantly improving our parser to prevent breakages and handle Canvas interfaces and formatted tables better.
  • Broader Obsidian Support: Optimizing the local-first writing workflow to let you save files directly to specific vaults and subfolders with less manual configuration.
  • Performance Enhancements: Improving the batch and history export reliability so large volume chats sync without hitting memory or rate limits.
