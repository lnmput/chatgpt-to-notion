Case Study: Building a Research Second Brain with Perplexity and Obsidian

How a professional academic researcher uses Perplexity's deep search engine and Obsidian's bi-directional linking to compile a local, private, and fully searchable second brain.

Install AI Chat Backup

The challenge: Capturing dynamic research

Dr. Elena Rostova is an independent researcher specializing in biotechnology. She relies heavily on **Perplexity AI** to perform literature reviews, find paper citations, and synthesize complex scientific queries. However, organizing this information was a bottleneck:

"Perplexity is fantastic for finding facts and links, but keeping 50 open browser tabs or copy-pasting answers into markdown notes was exhausting. I kept losing the source citation links and the tables would format incorrectly in Obsidian."

The workflow: From query to local knowledge base

By integrating **AI Chat Backup** into her daily stack, Elena established a frictionless three-step workflow to feed her Obsidian vault:

1. Curate and refine on Perplexity

Elena queries Perplexity to map out a research topic. Once the thread is fully loaded with search references and organized tables, she has the completed digest ready.

2. One-click local vault sync

With her Obsidian vault folder configured in AI Chat Backup's settings under **Markdown Export Folder**, Elena opens the side panel and clicks **Export Chat**. The extension immediately writes a `.md` file directly into her vault directory.

3. Metadata tagging and linking

Because the extension prepends YAML front matter to each export, Obsidian automatically indexes the note's metadata. Elena uses the Obsidian *Dataview* plugin to automatically query and catalog her research papers based on tags like `platform: perplexity` and custom date ranges.

Pro Tip: Dataview Integration. Enable YAML front matter in the extension settings. Inside Obsidian, you can query your research notes using a simple query: 
LIST FROM #research AND "AI Research/Perplexity"
---

Why this workflow beats copy-pasting

  • Citations are preserved: Perplexity's citation references (e.g. `[1]`, `[2]`) are converted into clickable hyperlinks at the bottom of the Markdown file, keeping sources verifiable.
  • Clean table formatting: Multi-column comparison tables are parsed into valid Markdown tables, rendering perfectly inside Obsidian's Preview Mode.
  • Local-first privacy: Since both the extension's Markdown compiler and Obsidian run locally, Elena’s unpublished research remains confidential and secure on her local hard drive.

Conclusion

By bridging Perplexity's search intelligence with Obsidian's local organization using AI Chat Backup, Dr. Rostova built a sustainable, scalable research archive. It saves hours of manual formatting weekly and guarantees her digital second brain stays organized.
