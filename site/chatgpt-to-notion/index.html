AI Chat Backup / ChatGPT to Notion

Notion sync guide

How to export ChatGPT conversations to Notion.

AI Chat Backup lets you choose a ChatGPT conversation and save it into an authorized Notion workspace. Use it for research, project decisions, code explanations, and any answer you expect to need after the original chat disappears from view.

Install AI Chat Backup

Connect your Notion workspace

  1. Install AI Chat Backup and open the extension popup.
  2. Select Connect to Notion.
  3. Sign in to the Notion account that owns the destination workspace.
  4. Choose the parent page the integration may access.
  5. Complete authorization and return to ChatGPT.
Notion access is scoped. The integration can only find pages shared during authorization. If the destination is missing, reauthorize and choose the correct parent page.

Save the current ChatGPT conversation

  1. Open a specific ChatGPT conversation and wait for the response to finish.
  2. Open AI Chat Backup and confirm that Notion shows as connected.
  3. Select the Notion sync action.
  4. Wait for the completion message before closing the ChatGPT tab.
  5. Open the authorized Notion page and review the saved content.

What the export preserves

The extension attempts to retain titles, prompts, responses, headings, lists, links, code blocks, and tables. ChatGPT canvases, temporary image URLs, interactive embeds, and newly introduced interface elements may require separate handling.

When Notion sync fails

  • Confirm that the selected parent page still exists.
  • Reauthorize after changing workspaces or revoking the integration.
  • Test one conversation before retrying a large batch.
  • Refresh ChatGPT if the conversation was still streaming.
  • Check the troubleshooting guide for current platform issues.

Privacy considerations

The extension sends conversation content to Notion only when you choose to sync it. Review the destination workspace permissions before saving sensitive material, and read the privacy policy for complete data-handling details.
