AI Chat Backup / Bulk export ChatGPT

Conversation history backup

Bulk export ChatGPT conversations with fewer failures.

The batch workflow scans compatible ChatGPT history pages and processes multiple conversations. A careful selection is more reliable than trying to export an entire large account in one run.

Install AI Chat Backup

Prepare the batch

  1. Update AI Chat Backup and reload ChatGPT.
  2. Open the compatible ChatGPT history or batch interface.
  3. Start with a small group of conversations.
  4. Choose the supported export destination.
  5. Keep the ChatGPT tab open while processing.

Why smaller batches work better

ChatGPT loads conversation data over the network and can change its interface without notice. Large histories increase the chance of temporary requests, rate limits, stale pages, or individual conversations with unusual content. Smaller batches make it easier to identify and retry one failure.

During the export

  • Do not reload or close the ChatGPT tab.
  • Avoid switching accounts during processing.
  • Keep the computer awake.
  • Wait for the completion or failure summary.
  • Retry failed conversations individually before repeating the whole batch.

What a backup should include

Store exported files in a folder with a separate backup policy. If the conversations matter to a project, copy that folder to encrypted cloud storage or another drive. A one-time export is not a complete backup strategy unless it exists in more than one location.
