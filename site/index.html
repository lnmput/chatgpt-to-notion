Before you export

Common questions.

Do I need Notion to use AI Chat Backup?+

No. Export Chat creates Markdown locally. You only need to authorize Notion when you want workspace sync.

Which AI platforms are supported?+

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Studio, Perplexity, Poe, and Grok are supported. Platform interface changes can temporarily affect extraction.

When does the extension read a conversation?+

It extracts content from the active supported chat when you choose Export Chat, Sync to Notion, or start a ChatGPT batch sync.

Can I sync multiple ChatGPT conversations?+

Yes. The Chrome side panel can sync the latest chats or conversations you select in the ChatGPT sidebar. Free batches are limited to five chats, and pinned items are skipped unless you include them.

What happens to AI-generated images?+

ChatGPT generated images can be downloaded separately from the extension. Notion sync can include their URLs, but cannot upload those generated files directly.
