Common questions

What to know before exporting.

Do I need Notion to use AI Chat Backup?

No. You can download Markdown or save files to a local folder without connecting a Notion account.

Which AI platforms are supported?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Studio, Perplexity, Poe, and Grok are supported. Platform interface changes can temporarily affect extraction.

Does the extension read my browsing history?

No. It operates on supported AI chat pages and processes conversation content when you choose an export or sync action.

Can I export multiple ChatGPT conversations?

The extension includes batch support for compatible ChatGPT history pages. Start with a smaller selection and keep the ChatGPT tab open during processing.
