AI Chat Backup / Gemini to Notion

Gemini export guide

Export Gemini conversations to Notion and Markdown.

Move useful Gemini research and responses into a durable workspace or local file without manually copying each section.

Install AI Chat Backup

Export the current Gemini chat

  1. Open the Gemini conversation you want to keep.
  2. Wait until the response and citations are visible.
  3. Open AI Chat Backup.
  4. Choose Notion sync, Markdown download, or a configured local folder.
  5. Review the exported links and media references.

Citations, links, and generated media

Gemini responses may include citations, expandable sections, and media whose URLs depend on the active session. Text and standard links are more portable than interactive or authenticated content. Save critical media separately when necessary.

Use Gemini exports in Notion

Add the exported conversation to the project page where it will be used. Summarize the decision or research outcome at the top so teammates do not need to reread the entire transcript.

Troubleshoot missing content

Expand collapsed content, wait for generation to complete, refresh the page, and retry. If Gemini changed its interface, update AI Chat Backup and check the public issue tracker.
